Pill drop

Half metal (heavy), half plastic (light), thrown from a roof.

Options
How the physics works 
weight     → pulls at the balance point (near the metal end)
fluid push → acts at the middle of the pill
middle is behind the balance point
           → fluid turns the metal end to the front
             (same reason a dart flies point-first)
ForceFormulaWins when
Pressure drag½·ρ·Cd·A·v²fast, thin fluid (air)
Viscous drag6π·μ·R·vslow, thick fluid (honey)
Buoyancyρ·V·g, updense fluid (water)

The Reynolds number Re = ρ·v·D/μ tells which drag wins. Above ~1000 pressure drag wins. Below ~1 viscous drag wins.

Drag is added up over 32 slices along the pill. So a tilted or spinning pill gets pushed harder on one side, and turns.

Not modelled: tumbling out of the screen plane (this is 2D), gusts, the fluid moving with the pill.
